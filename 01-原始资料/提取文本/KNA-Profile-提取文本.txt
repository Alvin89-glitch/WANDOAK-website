Founded in 2004, K&A has evolved into a leading ODM 
manufacturer in Eastern China. Our core philosophy, “To serve 
the best to the best,” drives us to help our clients excel through 
efficient, proactive, and rigorously controlled operations.
Our R&D strength spans complex pattern making, innovative 
technology integration, strategic material sourcing, and 
consistent quality assurance. With in‑house sourcing, 
development, and design teams, we deliver fresh fabric and 
accessory solutions to our customers every month. Regular 
participation in major international exhibitions keeps us at the 
forefront of global fashion trends.
We take pride in our professional service team, fluent in English 
and dedicated to seamless communication and responsive 
support. From sports bras, leggings, swimwear to beachwear, 
covering both cut and sewn as well as seamless construction, we 
turn concepts into market ready products that global brands 
trust. At K&A, we don't just manufacture; we empower success.
